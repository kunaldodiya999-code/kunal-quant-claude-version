# Research Gates

1. Causality — no future bars.
2. Data availability — input existed at decision time.
3. Determinism.
4. Execution realism.
5. Actual option economics, not only underlying proxies.
6. Robustness across periods and parameter perturbations.
7. Ablation against locked baseline.
8. Out-of-sample evaluation.
9. Backtest/Paper/Live operational consistency.
10. No silent changes to locked rules.
